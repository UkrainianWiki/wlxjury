section: Features

- Round form: one control for the files taken from the previous rounds, instead of the free "Number of top files" box and the "Average file rating is at least" field, which were the same cut. "Choose N" lists every cut that doesn't split a tie (files with the same score tie) as "top 88 · score ≥ 8.0" (for yes/no rounds, the lowest vote level taken), the candidate cuts first with their reasons (a clear drop in scores, all candidates, the edges of a tie), and "No top N". From rated rounds the round keeps the files scoring at least the cut's score (the default) or exactly the top N; a number inside a tie is refused on save, and checked again when the files are chosen. "Show this cut in the ratings" opens the previous round's ratings at the cut, with a line between the last file taken and the first left out, where those ratings list the same files in the same order. A round saved earlier keeps its settings; re-applying its filters preselects its cut or asks for one. How the cut was chosen is kept in the round's settings log (#237).
