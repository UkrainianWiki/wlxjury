# WLX Jury user guide

The guide for the people who use WLX Jury in a Wiki Loves Monuments, Wiki Loves Earth or similar
photo contest. It is a set of short topic pages. Each role has a contents page that lists the pages
it needs, in order:

- [Juror guide](juror-guide.md): for jurors, who vote on the files of their rounds.
- [Organizer guide](organizer-guide.md): for organizers, who follow the voting and read the
  results.
- [Admin guide](admin-guide.md): for a contest's admins, who set it up and run the judging, and
  for root, the tool's own administrators.

[What's new in 2026 (up to version 26.11)](whats-new-26.11.md) lists the changes of the year.

Labels in **bold** are the ones the tool shows in English. The pages are in English; in the tool,
the same buttons and menus are in the same places in every language.

## All the pages

**For everyone**

- [Signing in](signing-in.md): invitations, passwords, signing out and sessions; for admins,
  setting another person's password; for root, API tokens.
- [Your settings](settings.md): the theme, the interface language, your profile, and the
  supported browsers.
- [Common problems](troubleshooting.md): expired pages, "Access denied", being signed out, other
  errors.

**For jurors**

- [Finding your rounds](juror-rounds.md): what you see after signing in, rounds you don't see,
  several contests, and the kinds of rounds.
- [The gallery](juror-gallery.md): its top bar and menus, comments, sorting, the pager, and voting
  in the gallery.
- [The large view](juror-large-view.md): one file large, the monument's page, voting and rating by
  criteria, keyboard shortcuts, comments.
- [What you can and can't see](juror-visibility.md): your own votes only, conflicts of interest,
  disqualified files.

**For organizers and admins**

- [Accounts and roles](accounts.md): one account per person, the roles, who can do what, the
  current contest.
- [Following the voting](statistics.md): where organizers start, and a round's statistics.
- [Results](results.md): the results of all jurors or one, the by-rate gallery, the file list, the
  large view of all jurors, the CSV.
- [Comments](comments.md): files with comments, the comments report, one person's comments.
- [Scores and the worst mark](scoring.md): how a rated round scores its files, and the worst mark.
- [The contest's files](contest-files.md): the contest files page; for admins, importing files.
- [Monuments and authors](monuments-authors.md): a monument's page, the files' authors and the
  authors list.
- [Conflicts of interest](conflicts.md): what they are, the conflicts page; for admins, leaving
  them out of a round and disqualifying files.

**For admins**

- [People](people.md): the users page, adding people one by one, from a list or from another
  contest.
- [Changing people's accounts](editing-people.md): changing a person, the account changes, possible
  duplicate accounts and joining two accounts.
- [Invitations](invitations.md): the invitation email, the invitations list, links to share, the
  greeting.
- [The contest](contest.md): contest settings, series and series admins, several admins.
- [Running the judging](rounds.md): a contest's rounds, the kinds of rounds, the life of a round,
  the rounds list, starting and stopping, what jurors see.
- [Creating and changing a round](round-create.md): the round form, the distribution of files,
  changing a round.
- [Choosing a round's files](round-files.md): the filters, earlier rounds' votes, the rejection
  filter, adding files by name, distributing new files.
- [Reviewing a round's images](round-review.md): checking a new round's files before the jurors
  get them.
- [A round's jurors](round-jurors.md): choosing them, pausing a juror, adding a juror, reassigning
  a juror's unrated files.
- [Tracks](tracks.md): how the tool finds a round's track, and the track label.

**For root**

- [What only root can do](root.md): contests, series, every account, and normalizing stored
  authors.
